Photosensitivity warning

This page is a strobe. Once you press Start it flashes, and flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, do not start it.

Nothing moves or flashes until you press Start. It starts on the plugin's own settings: one slice at a time, white, two steps a second at 120 BPM, which is under the three flashes a second that broadcast and web guidance (Ofcom's, and WCAG's) puts as the limit for a large flash. Some controls go much faster. The line under the picture says how often the busiest slice is flashing, and warns past three a second. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
